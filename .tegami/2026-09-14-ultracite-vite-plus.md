Upgrade Ultracite from 7.10.8 to 7.11.1. Require Oxlint ^1.82.0 and support Oxfmt ^0.67.0 or ^0.70.0, including the versions bundled with Vite+ 1.0. Build the formatter preset against Oxfmt 0.70.0. Existing shared rule overrides remain in place.
