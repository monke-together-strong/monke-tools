## Make mt the canonical local command

Local installs now place the executable at ~/.local/bin/mt, keep monke as a wrapper, and remove obsolete monke-tools artifacts. Shell integration now falls back to mt.

The shared Oxc config permits warning comments such as TODO and FIXME, and recurring-issue diagnosis guidance now leads with mechanism-level causal chains and ordered next steps.
